License: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3)
Repository: https://github.com/Voice-Lab/VoiceLab
Citation: Feinberg, D. (2022). VoiceLab: Software for Fully Reproducible
          Automated Voice Analysis. Proc. Interspeech 2022, 351-355.

The acoustic analysis algorithms in this project are based on VoiceLab v2.0.0.
VoiceLab wraps Praat's C/C++ algorithms via parselmouth-praat in a modular
node-based pipeline. The measurement implementations follow VoiceLab's approach
for pitch, jitter, shimmer, HNR, CPP, formants, LTAS, spectral tilt, and
spectral shape analysis.
